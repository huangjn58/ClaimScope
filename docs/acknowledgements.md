# Acknowledgements and provenance

ClaimScope develops a modular framework for scientific claims, evidence, scope,
paper narrative, thesis-section logic and reviewer-response workflows. It builds on
a local source skill named `academic-writing`; that source is not part of this public
distribution and is not renamed or modified by the installers.

## Intellectual sources

- [Adkid-Zephyr/anti-defensive-writing-Skill](https://github.com/Adkid-Zephyr/anti-defensive-writing-Skill):
  the Press-Release Principle and contribution-centered organization.
- [Kiterlin/anti-defensive-writing](https://github.com/Kiterlin/anti-defensive-writing):
  distinguishing unnecessary defensive writing from necessary scientific and
  methodological boundaries.

ClaimScope reorganizes and adapts these ideas rather than simply concatenating their
instructions. It explicitly prioritizes evidence consistency over narrative, protects
decisive negative results, separates writing from new research commitments, and supplies
section-specific workflows and fictional examples. Attribution does not imply upstream
endorsement. No private research manuscripts or data were used as public examples.

Both upstream LICENSE files were checked on 2026-09-28 and contained the MIT notices
below. These are preserved conservatively for adapted guidance. The installable skill
also carries a LICENSE so attribution is not lost when it is copied independently.
