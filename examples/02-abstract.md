# Problem

Fictional teaching example with stipulated values: a laboratory sensor was calibrated
at three temperatures. Mean absolute error was 4 units before correction and 2 units
after correction on those same calibration observations. No independent validation
or other temperatures were tested. These numbers are invented for this example only.

# Original

We performed sensor tests at three temperatures and then applied a correction.
The mean absolute error changed from 4 to 2 units on the calibration observations.
This establishes a universally accurate sensor system, although much work remains.

# Diagnosis

The result supports improved fit on calibration observations, not universal accuracy
or independent predictive performance. The activity log and generic closing hide that distinction.

# Rewrite

This study evaluates a temperature-dependent sensor correction at three laboratory
temperatures. On the calibration observations, the correction reduced mean absolute
error from 4 to 2 units. The result supports correction of the measured temperature
dependence within the calibration conditions; independent predictive accuracy was not assessed.

# Why this works

Preserves the exact numbers and identifies the evidence type. The final qualifier is
necessary in this abstract because otherwise the performance claim could be read as
independently validated. It is not removed merely to create a stronger headline.
