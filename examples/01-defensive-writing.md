# Problem

Fictional teaching example: a draft weakens a measured relationship with empty apologies.
The stipulated evidence is a consistent X–Y association under the tested conditions,
not a causal mechanism or universal law. No real study or private data are represented.

# Original

Although useful observations were obtained, it must be acknowledged that only the
investigated loading conditions were considered. The observations show a consistent
relationship between X and Y, but the results should be interpreted cautiously.

# Diagnosis

The observed relationship is the claim; investigated loading conditions are necessary
scope. The apologies and unspecified caution add no information under the stated premise.

# Rewrite

Under the investigated loading conditions, the observations show a consistent
relationship between X and Y.

# Why this works

The association remains an association. The scope is explicit, and the contribution
is no longer buried. If a real draft contains a particular measurement uncertainty,
that uncertainty must remain rather than being treated as generic caution.
