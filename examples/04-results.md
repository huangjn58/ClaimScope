# Problem

Fictional teaching example: measured error decreased in condition A but increased in B.
Both conditions are primary outcomes. No microscopic structural measurement exists.

# Original

We first measured condition A and then condition B. Error decreased in A but increased
in B. Unfortunately, this shows that the whole method is inadequate, although the
improvement in A clearly proves a structural mechanism.

# Diagnosis

The draft combines an activity log, an overbroad negative assessment and unsupported
mechanistic proof. The adverse primary result must stay in the main comparison.

# Rewrite

Error decreased in condition A and increased in condition B, showing a condition-dependent
response rather than a uniform improvement across the two primary outcomes.

# Why this works

Keeps the unfavorable result and narrows the performance claim. It does not pretend
that the outcome proves a microscopic mechanism or that one adverse result condemns
every possible use of the method.
