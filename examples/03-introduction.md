# Problem

Fictional teaching example: the supplied literature summary covers constant-temperature
response but leaves transient temperature response unresolved for the stated material.
No bibliographic identifiers are invented here; a real manuscript must retain its verified citations.

# Original

Previous researchers have failed to solve this important problem. We first assembled
an apparatus and then carried out transient-temperature tests. Our study is limited
and does not attempt to answer every question about this material.

# Diagnosis

The gap concerns a particular transient response, not the general worth of prior work.
Equipment assembly is a method detail. The contribution should be stated as an objective,
not a completed finding without results.

# Rewrite

Existing studies characterize the material under constant temperature, while its
response to transient temperature changes remains unresolved in the supplied literature.
This study examines that response through transient-temperature tests.

# Why this works

Connects prior knowledge, a bounded gap and the research objective without fabricated
novelty. In a real introduction, replace the reference to the supplied literature with
the verified literature synthesis and its existing citations, not invented references.
