# Problem

Fictional teaching example: all tested specimens retained residual deformation after
unloading; the manuscript has already reported this observation. No microscopic
mechanism or untested material class was established.

# Original

All tested specimens retained residual deformation after unloading. This proves the
universal microscopic mechanism. Despite these useful achievements, the study has
many shortcomings and extensive future work remains necessary.

# Diagnosis

The observation supports incomplete recovery in the tested specimens. Universal
microscopic proof is unsupported; the generic disclaimer supplies no actual boundary.

# Rewrite

All tested specimens retained residual deformation after unloading, demonstrating
incomplete recovery under the investigated conditions.

# Why this works

The conclusion is stronger in organization, not broader in factual reach. It answers
the observed recovery question without adding mechanisms or new research commitments.
