# Problem

Fictional teaching example: treatment and response are associated in an observational
dataset. Baseline exposure was not controlled and may confound that association.
No randomized intervention was performed.

# Original

The treatment may perhaps have some influence on the response. It is worth noting
that baseline exposure was not controlled, although this probably does not matter.

# Diagnosis

The association can be stated directly, but a causal interpretation is not identified.
The known potential confounder cannot be dismissed without evidence.

# Rewrite

Treatment was associated with the response in the observational dataset. Because
baseline exposure was not controlled, the association does not isolate a causal
treatment effect.

# Why this works

Replaces vague hedging with a precise inference boundary. “Does not isolate” performs
a real methodological function and is retained despite being a negative construction.
