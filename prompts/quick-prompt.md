# Quick prompts

Append your authorized text and relevant evidence after a prompt. Examples are not
permission to fabricate missing facts. Request audit-only mode when you do not want edits.

## Thesis revision

Use $claimscope to revise this PhD thesis paragraph. Preserve all data and scientific
meaning. Improve claim–evidence alignment and remove unnecessary defensive framing.

## Defensive-writing audit

Use $claimscope to identify unnecessary defensive writing. Separate real scientific
boundaries from redundant caveats. Classify their function. Do not rewrite yet.

## Abstract

Use $claimscope to revise this abstract around the scientific problem, method, key
findings and contribution. Preserve necessary scope and all quantitative information.

## Introduction

Use $claimscope to connect the scientific question, verified literature gap and
contribution. Do not invent novelty or disparage prior work.

## Literature review

Use $claimscope to organize this review by scientific questions and evidence gaps,
preserving citations and distinguishing established findings from unresolved issues.

## Results

Use $claimscope to lead with the observed findings and their quantitative support.
Keep uncertainty and adverse results; separate observations from mechanism inference.

## Discussion

Use $claimscope to strengthen the mechanism discussion. If the evidence does not
support a broad claim, narrow the claim instead of adding vague hedging. Do not invent mechanisms.

## Conclusions

Use $claimscope to answer the scientific question with the strongest supported
findings. Add no new results or generic self-critical ending.

## Reviewer response

Use $claimscope to respond to this reviewer comment. First determine whether the
existing evidence is sufficient. Do not propose new experiments unless the claim
truly cannot be supported otherwise; identify the gap first and obtain my authorization
before proposing or committing to expanded research. Do not invent completed revisions.

## Conservative revision

Use $claimscope for minimal language edits. Preserve structure, facts, terminology,
equations, citations and uncertainty. Flag scientific inconsistencies rather than silently changing them.

## Deep restructuring

Use $claimscope to reorganize the argument around its strongest supported contribution.
Preserve decisive counterevidence, research objectives and required reporting.

## Scope audit

Use $claimscope to assess each claim against its evidence and application range.
Identify overstatement, necessary qualification and missing evidence. Do not rewrite yet.
